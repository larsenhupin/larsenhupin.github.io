Fading LEDs using 555 Timer on Breadboard

Jul 18, 2025

Fading leds

A common circuit for beginner is flashing a LED. Here’s a varient with a flashing component. This article describe how to make the circuit on a breadboard.


Tools

General tools

Plier cutter, non-serrated long nose plier, wire stripper. Open image on a new tab

Multimeter

The multimeter is an essential tool in electronics. It’s useful to capture voltage in your circuit.
It can be use to test the components before building the circuit. Most models are able to test resistors, capacitors and LEDs.

Power supply

Use a switching power supply or a 5V portable battery. I have a variable switching power supply that I cutted the wire and installed Dupont connector at the end. If you have a portable 5V battery, you can cut a USB cable and connect the power and ground to the Dupont connector.

Material

Component Description
Breadboard Use quality breadboard. This one is from Elegoo
Solid wire Make sure it's not stranded wire. 22 AWG is a good size
555 timer IC Classic
0.1 µF ceramic capacitor Placed across the power line to debounce overshooting above 5V
0.01 µF ceramic capacitor Connected between GND and PIN 5 to reduce noise
24 µF electrolytic capacitor Connected between GND and PIN 2
NPN transistor 2N2222
1KΩ resistor Connected between 5V and PIN 7
5KΩ resistor Connected between PIN 6 and PIN 7
100KΩ resistor Connected between PIN 3 and transistor Base
330Ω resistors (6) Connected between LED Cathode and GND
Red LEDs (6)
